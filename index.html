About Us

Built by a Golfer,
Engineered with Precision

Sarko Nalband spent years as an electrical engineer and financial analyst before golf took over completely. After becoming obsessed with the game, he developed the Sim Sigma system — a data-driven approach to simulator training that helped him increase swing speed by 50% and drop his scoring average by 15 strokes in just a few months.

Today he brings that same analytical precision to every build. Sarko personally designs and oversees each installation, combining deep technical expertise with a golfer's eye to deliver simulator experiences that are dialed in from the ground up.

Based in Los Angeles, he works with clients across Southern California who are serious about their game.
